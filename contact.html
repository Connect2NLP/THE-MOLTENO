Meet your host, Julian

So, this is Julian

Your gracious, fun host at The Molteno

Julian, or as many know him, Jules, your host at The Molteno, is a social, attentive, fun guy who brings heart, laughter, and a genuine love for people into every guest experience. With a passion for hospitality, entertaining, food & wine, the outdoors, plants, music, and creating meaningful connections, Jules ensures every stay feels personal, comfortable, and truly memorable.

He loves a good cook-up, and hosting intimate guest dinners, braais and social gatherings. He'll go the extra mile for people and is great at maintaining quality connections and friendships long after the stay at The Molteno.

FAQ's

Frequently asked questions

Do the rooms have views of Table Mountain?

Several of our rooms, including the Monarch Suite and Panorama Suite, offer direct views of Table Mountain.

Is parking available at The Molteno?

Yes, secure parking can be arranged for guests — just let us know ahead of your stay.

How far is The Molteno from Cape Town's main attractions?

We're just minutes from Kloof Street, Company's Garden and the Table Mountain cableway.

Is Wi-Fi available throughout the property?

Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi covers the whole property.

Is The Molteno suitable for couples or solo travelers?

Absolutely — our rooms suit solo travellers, couples, and everyone in between.
