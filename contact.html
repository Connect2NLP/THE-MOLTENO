FAQ's

Frequently asked questions

Do the rooms have views of Table Mountain?

Several of our rooms, including the Monarch Suite and Panorama Suite, offer direct views of Table Mountain.

Is parking available at The Molteno?

Yes, secure parking can be arranged for guests — just let us know ahead of your stay.

How far is The Molteno from Cape Town's main attractions?

We're just minutes from Kloof Street, Company's Garden and the Table Mountain cableway.

Is Wi-Fi available throughout the property?

Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi covers the whole property.

Is The Molteno suitable for couples or solo travelers?

Absolutely — our rooms suit solo travellers, couples, and everyone in between.
