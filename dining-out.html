Julian’s Favourite & Suggested Restaurants

Discover Cape Town through its food, atmosphere, and exceptional dining experiences.

At The Molteno, we believe that discovering a destination is as much about its food and people as it is about its landmarks. Julian has curated this selection of favourite and recommended restaurants to help you find everything from relaxed neighbourhood dining and lively social spots to memorable seafood, international cuisine, and special evenings out.

Whether you are looking for a casual lunch, a romantic dinner, a lively evening with friends, or a restaurant with beautiful ocean views, this guide will help you find a place to suit the occasion.

Enjoy exploring Cape Town, one restaurant at a time.
