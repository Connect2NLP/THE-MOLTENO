Curated Experiences

At The Molteno, your stay is our highest priority. Every experience is thoughtfully arranged to ensure you enjoy Cape Town in style, comfort, and at your own pace. From moments of quiet indulgence to unforgettable adventures, everything is tailored to make you feel truly special.

Getting Around

Experienced Drivers

See the city through the eyes of someone who knows it best. Our trusted and highly experienced tour-guide chauffeurs offer airport transfers, private tours, and scenic drives. Always on time, and just pleasant and professional. From the Winelands to Cape Point, they'll show you all the spectacular hidden gems and local favourites.

  • Airport transfers and city tours
  • Winelands excursions
  • Scenic coastal drives
  • Local knowledge and recommendations
Fitness

Step outside and take your fitness to the next level with Fred, our expert personal trainer. Offering 1:1 or couples sessions just across the house or right at the Atlantic ocean. Fred brings energy, expertise, and motivation to every workout, making fitness feel effortless, fun, and inspiring.

  • Personalized fitness programs
  • One-on-one training sessions
  • Nutrition guidance
  • Advance booking
Sport

Tennis with a View

Just a few steps from our front door, you'll find beautiful tennis courts framed by the iconic Table Mountain. We'll help you book a court and supply rackets. All you have to do is enjoy the game and the view.

  • Equipment available on request
