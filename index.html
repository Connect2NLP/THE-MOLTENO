The Story

A home away from home in the Mother City

Perfectly positioned just minutes from Cape Town's most celebrated attractions — from Kloof Street's café culture to the iconic Company's Garden and Table Mountain's cableway — The Molteno offers effortless access to the city's best restaurants, vibrant rooftop bars and many art galleries.

Something for everyone, wrapped in Victorian charm and warm, personal hospitality.

FAQ's

Ask away

Do you offer parking?

Yes, secure parking is available for guests — please let us know ahead of your stay so we can arrange it.

Where is The Molteno located?

We're at 4 Molteno Road, Gardens — minutes from Kloof Street, Company's Garden and the Table Mountain cableway.

Is there Wi-Fi available?

Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

Can I schedule a call with the host?

Absolutely — reach out via the contact page and we'll arrange a time that suits you.

How many rooms does the house have and could I book the entire space?

The Molteno has five individually designed rooms and suites. Full-house buyouts can be arranged on request.
