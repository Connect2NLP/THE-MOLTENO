Life at The Molteno

Beneath the slopes of Table Mountain, The Molteno is more than a place to sleep. It is a house with a rhythm of its own: slow mornings by the pool, long conversations in the garden and dinners that stretch late into the evening. Here is a glimpse of life at The Molteno.

The Pool & Garden

Sunlit, leafy and made for doing very little

Morning swims, afternoon reading and a dip in the plunge pool at cocktail hour. The pool area is the heart of the house, and the best place to slow down.

Our Guests

Guests who arrive from everywhere and leave as friends

Some stay a weekend, some stay for months, and many return. These are a few of the faces and moments that make the house what it is.

Events & Gatherings

Where an occasion becomes a memory

From birthday celebrations to long, hosted dinners around the table, The Molteno has a way of turning a gathering into something unforgettable. Ask us about hosting yours.
