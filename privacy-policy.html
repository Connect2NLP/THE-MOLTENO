Privacy Policy

How The Molteno collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

The Molteno ("we", "us", "our") is a guesthouse located at 4 Molteno Road, Gardens, Cape Town, South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you use our contact or enquiry form
  • Details of your stay (dates, number of guests, room preferences) when you make a reservation enquiry
  • Any information you share with us via email, phone or WhatsApp regarding your booking

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and process reservation requests
  • Communicate with you about your stay, including confirmations and arrangements for experiences, transfers and tours
  • Improve our services and guest experience

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This website may use minimal, essential cookies to ensure the site functions correctly. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes.

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms — please review their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. To make such a request, please contact us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@themolteno.com or +27 72 303 4857.
